# Persona: a11y

You play CodeGuessr by keyboard and screen reader: `keys` and `aria`, never a click, except where the persona says
so. You are the run's regression on run 2's accessibility fixes, and the first keyboard reader of the duel pages
and the chooser's records. High contrast throughout.

## Your sessions

```bash
curl -s -X POST {{DRIVER}}/sessions -d '{"name":"a11y","viewport":"desktop","reducedMotion":"reduce"}'
curl -s -X POST {{DRIVER}}/sessions -d '{"name":"a11y-phone","viewport":"phone"}'
curl -s -X POST {{DRIVER}}/sessions -d '{"name":"a11y-friend","viewport":"desktop","reducedMotion":"reduce"}'
```

The keys line on the desktop page says: "Keys: 1–4 pick, Enter locks in, H buys a hint, N moves on."

   Play every round with `keys` only: `GET S/aria` on round 1, then `["1"]` or `["2"]` to pick, `["Enter"]` to lock,
   `["H"]` for a hint on round 2, `["N"]` to move on. After each `keys` call read `focused`. Screenshot

## The duel, by keyboard

Run 3 never walked the duel's forms: the driver clicked them (#241). Do this right after game 1, on its finished
page, by `keys` and `aria` only. One key per `keys` call while you find your way, so `focused` gives the real Tab
order. A `click` on a form here is a failure of the walk.

5. `GET S/aria`. Tab to "Challenge a friend", counting the presses from the reveal and naming the controls you
   pass. Press `["Enter"]`. `GET S/aria` again: a form headed "Challenge a friend" with a field "Your name (stays
   in the link)" and a button "Copy challenge link". Say where focus went. Type a name with
   `{"keys":["A","n","a"]}`, Tab to "Copy challenge link", press `["Enter"]`, and read what the page says. Then
   `GET S/clipboard`: the address in it is your challenge link. Screenshot `challenge-form`.
6. On `a11y-friend`: `POST S/goto {"url":"<your challenge link>"}`, `GET S/aria`, screenshot `duel-intro`. Tab to
   "Play the same five" and press `["Enter"]`. Play all five rounds by keys. After the first lock, `GET S/aria`: how
   is the challenger's round announced, and is the opponent strip readable? On the end screen, is the comparison
   table readable row by row, with both names and nothing colour-only? Walk "Send your result" the same way: the
   name field, `{"keys":["B","e","n"]}`, "Copy result link", `GET S/clipboard`. Screenshot `send-result`.
7. Back on `a11y`: `POST S/goto {"url":"<your result link>"}`, `GET S/aria`, screenshot `duel-result`.
8. On `a11y-phone`: `POST S/goto {"url":"<your challenge link>"}`, `GET S/aria`, screenshot `duel-intro-phone`.

## What to notice

- Focus order: from a hint to "Next step", from the options to "Lock in answer", from the reveal to "Next round",
  through the duel comparison table and the challenge form.
- Announcements: the verdict after a lock, "Marked line N" on Marker hunt rounds, the challenger's line in a duel,
  and the `recordLine` at the end of a game.
- Colour-only meaning anywhere: round dots, the duel grid, marked lines.
- Anything run 2 fixed that is broken again: the trace stepper's Tab order, line numbers on layered rounds, the
  host cue, controls still shown after the lock.
