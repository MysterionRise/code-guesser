# Persona: a11y

You play CodeGuessr by keyboard and screen reader: `keys` and `aria`, never a click, except where the persona says
so. You are the run's regression on run 2's accessibility fixes, and the first keyboard reader of the duel pages
and the chooser's records. High contrast throughout.

## Your sessions

```bash
curl -s -X POST {{DRIVER}}/sessions -d '{"name":"a11y","viewport":"desktop","reducedMotion":"reduce"}'
curl -s -X POST {{DRIVER}}/sessions -d '{"name":"a11y-phone","viewport":"phone"}'
```

The keys line on the desktop page says: "Keys: 1–4 pick, Enter locks in, H buys a hint, N moves on."

## Your games, in this order

1. On `a11y`: `POST S/start {"deck":"before-after","deal":"demo","game":0,"design":"off","theme":"contrast","layer":"off"}`.
   Play every round with `keys` only: `GET S/aria` on round 1, then `["1"]` or `["2"]` to pick, `["Enter"]` to lock,
   `["H"]` for a hint on round 2, `["N"]` to move on. After each `keys` call read `focused`. Screenshot
   `ba-keyboard-r2`. At the end, `GET S/aria` on the summary.
2. On `a11y`: `POST S/start {"deck":"algorithm","deal":"demo","game":0,"design":"visualizer","theme":"contrast","layer":"on"}`.
   Round 1: `aria`, then `["H"]`, `["H"]` (the second hint is a step-through), then Tab to "Next step" and press it
   twice with `["Enter"]`, reading `focused` each time. Lock in with keys. Then `POST S/explain` (the one click
   allowed), `GET S/aria`, and Tab into the visualizer's frame; say where focus went. Screenshot `algo-visualizer`.
   Play the rest by keys.
3. On `a11y-phone`: `POST S/start {"deck":"language","deal":"demo","game":0,"design":"off","theme":"contrast","layer":"on"}`.
   Round 1: buy hint 1 (marked lines) with `POST S/hint`, then `GET S/aria` and find how the marked lines are
   announced. Play the rest with `keys` where the phone page allows it; note that the keys line is hidden and
   whether the keys still work.

## Pages

4. On `a11y`: `POST S/goto {"url":"/"}`, `GET S/aria`. Are the records on the cards ("Best … · streak …", "Day
   streak …") readable in order, and is "Clear records" reachable? (This session has records from games 1 and 2.)
5. `POST S/goto {"url":"{{CHALLENGE_LINK}}"}`, `GET S/aria`, screenshot `duel-intro`. Tab to "Play the same five"
   and press it. On round 1, `GET S/aria`: how is the opponent strip announced? Lock in round 1 by keys and read the
   challenger's line in the live region. Stop after round 1.
6. `POST S/goto {"url":"{{RESULT_LINK}}"}`, `GET S/aria`, screenshot `duel-result`. Is the comparison table
   readable row by row, with both names, and nothing colour-only?

## What to notice

- Focus order: from a hint to "Next step", from the options to "Lock in answer", from the reveal to "Next round",
  through the duel comparison table and the challenge form.
- Announcements: the verdict after a lock, "Marked line N" on Marker hunt rounds, the challenger's line in a duel,
  and the `recordLine` at the end of a game.
- Colour-only meaning anywhere: round dots, the duel grid, marked lines.
- Anything run 2 fixed that is broken again: the trace stepper's Tab order, line numbers on layered rounds, the
  host cue, controls still shown after the lock.
